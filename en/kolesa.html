How it works

A preset opens in the same wheel through a link like index.html?preset=eda. The options appear right away but are not saved in your browser: as long as you change nothing, your previous list stays where it was. Start editing and the new list is what gets saved.

If the set you need is not there, open the wheel and type your own options — one at a time or several separated by commas. The list is remembered in your browser.
