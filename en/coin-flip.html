    How the online coin flip works

    This coin works like a real one: it has two sides, and each lands with a 50% chance. The result comes from the browser’s random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues), so there is no way to rig the toss or predict it.

    It is handy for settling a quick bet, drawing lots, deciding who buys the coffee, or flipping a coin when you do not have one in your pocket. Every toss is counted in the statistics, so you can see how often heads and tails come up.

    Statistics stay in your browser and are never sent anywhere. The “Clear statistics” button wipes the counters and the flip history.

    Need to choose between more than two options? Open the spin wheel and add your own list.
